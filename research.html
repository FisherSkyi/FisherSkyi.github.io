Research

Current and recent work

My work is centered on dependable machine learning, especially selective prediction, learning-to-defer, and robust decision-making systems.

L2D in Time Series

Extending the learning-to-defer framework to time series data.

Developing a new architecture that handles bandit feedback from selected experts.

Code Base: GitHub Repository

Collaborator & Supervisor: Yannis Montreuil [NUS & CNRS]

arXiv Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22538

[AISTATS 2026] Adversarially Robust Learning-to-Defer for Classification and Regression

Extending the robustness framework of Montreuil et al. (2025a) to the one-stage setting by modeling the interaction between the predictor and adaptive experts.

Code Base: Currently Not Public

Collaborator & Supervisor: Yannis Montreuil [NUS & CNRS]

arXiv Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10988

Road Sign Classification with Denoising Pipeline Approach

Real-world images are affected by noise, which reduces the reliability of traffic sign recognition. This work evaluates a denoiser-classifier pipeline against an end-to-end approach on GTSRB under Gaussian noise.

Code Base: classify_denoise_SwinIR
